FAQ

How accurate is the data?

All salary data comes from the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) median salary figures by occupation. All cost data comes from the Department of Education IPEDS and College Scorecard datasets. Federal student loan rates use the current standard 10-year repayment terms.

What does Premium include?

The free version includes 2 full ROI calculations. Premium ($5.99, one-time payment) unlocks unlimited calculations, all 75+ majors, and the side-by-side comparison tool. No subscription — you pay once and keep it forever.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, navigate to the paywall screen, and tap "Restore Purchase" at the bottom. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on any device signed into the same account.

Does the app require an internet connection?

All ROI calculations work offline. An internet connection is only needed for the initial in-app purchase or purchase restoration.

What devices are supported?

CollegeROI supports iPhone and iPad running iOS 16.0 or later.
