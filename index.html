FAQ

How accurate is the data?

All salary data comes from the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) and all cost data from the Department of Education (IPEDS/College Scorecard). We use the most recent government data available.

What does Premium include?

The free version includes 2 full ROI calculations. Premium ($5.99, one-time payment) unlocks unlimited calculations, all majors, and the side-by-side comparison tool. No subscription required.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, tap the paywall screen, and tap "Restore Purchase" at the bottom. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on any device.
