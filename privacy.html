Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

Overview

CollegeROI ("the App") is developed by Florin Radu. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data.

CollegeROI does not:

  • Require an account or sign-in
  • Collect your name, email, or contact information
  • Track your location
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use cookies or similar technologies
  • Access your device's camera, microphone, contacts, or photos

On-Device Processing

All calculations and data processing happen entirely on your device. The App does not connect to any external servers or APIs. Your inputs (school type, major, cost, loan terms) are never transmitted off your device.

In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Purchase transactions are managed by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Data Storage

The App stores your premium purchase status locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data is not accessible to us or any third party.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal data from any user, the App is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
